Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Shiver Reels web app and the small publishing tools run by the same owner, including an uploader that posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page.

What we collect

Nothing about visitors is stored on any server we run. Shiver Reels runs entirely in your browser. When you sign in with Pollinations, the key Pollinations gives the app stays in your browser tab only and is used only to send your requests to Pollinations.

The uploader only accesses the owner's own accounts. It uses Google's YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's name, and it uses Meta's APIs to publish to the owner's own Instagram account and Facebook Page and read comments on those posts. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared or sold.

Google user data

The uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to upload videos and confirm the destination channel. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Third parties

Generation requests go to Pollinations under its own terms. Posts go to YouTube, Instagram and Facebook under their terms.
